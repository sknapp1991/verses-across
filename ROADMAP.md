# Verses Across roadmap

The world's scriptures side by side, in many languages. Free, no ads, no account. Live at https://versesacross.com.
This file is the source of truth; the roadmap page https://claude.ai/artifact/CVVQrFYEPTTRCA4gD85vCE (shortcuts: Desktop\Verses Across Roadmap and Desktop\Magnum Opus\Verses Across Files\Verses Across Roadmap; the Nov 7 page G3QttiDx… is retired) shows the same.

Key facts: 111 versions of 78 works · 15 languages · 18 sections · about 27,600 chapters · open source (AGPL-3.0) ·
Cloudflare Workers + R2 · pushing to `main` deploys · every change reviewed by Fable before it ships.

**Sister site:** the classic books (Literature, Freethought, Psychology) moved to **Library Across** (https://libraryacross.com) on Nov 10, built from this same code. Its roadmap is `ROADMAP-LIBRARY.md` (the hub's Roadmap tab has a switch).
