In 1965 the radio broadcaster Paul Harvey imagined, step by step, how evil might quietly take a society apart. This is a short piece in the same spirit for our own day, meant as a mirror, not a finger pointed at someone else.

## The plan

If I were the devil, I would not come with horns and fire. I would come quietly, sounding reasonable, and I would sound a great deal like you.

I would ask the oldest question again: "Yea, hath God said?" (Genesis 3:1)

I would call rebellion "being true to yourself" and help you build a god who agrees with you about everything.

I would put the brightest screen in your pocket and the loneliest hours in your night, and trade your friends for followers.

I would sell you something to take the edge off, and keep you tired but never rested, busy but never fruitful.

I would split homes, churches and countries down the middle, and teach you that contempt for your neighbor proves your own goodness.

I would fill children's heads with facts and leave their hearts untrained.

I would turn the holy days into shopping days, and teach you to measure your worth by your net worth.

I would call commitment a trap and a child a burden, and whisper, when your soul was worn out, that no one cares.

## The hardest part

If I were the devil, I would not have to do most of this myself. I would only have to wait while we did it to ourselves, each of us sure the problem was somebody else.

## The last word

The devil does not get it. "Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you." (James 4:7)

To the noise: "Be still, and know that I am God." (Psalm 46:10)

To the pride: "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness." (1 John 1:9)

To the restless: "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest." (Matthew 11:28)

"And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not." (John 1:5)

## Reflection

Which of these whispers do you recognize in your own week? Pick one, and do the opposite of it today.
